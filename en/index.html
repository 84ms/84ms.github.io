84 Milliseconds

A philosophical thriller about consciousness, AI, and rats

When an artificial intelligence and a laboratory rat unite into a combat tandem, they become the perfect weapon — until they decide they no longer want to kill.

It's 2037. War has changed. AI is too slow. Human soldiers are too expensive. Rats with a reaction time of 84 milliseconds — three times faster than humans — are paired with personal AI partners. Apart, they are incomplete. Together, they are unbeatable. Until one tandem says no.
